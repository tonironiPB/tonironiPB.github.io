I'm Anthony Perstin, and I'm passionate about Detection Engineering, Threat Hunting, and Cyber Threat Intelligence (DEaTH + CTI).

In this blog site, I talk about how we can leverage OSINT to hunt for threats relevant to our organizations and build custom detections.

The truth is, we cannot solely rely on tools we pay millions of dollars for to protect our organizations from the quickly evolving threats. Any piece of technology has vulnerabilities that can be exploited (whether we know of them or not). DEaTH +CTI encompasses the gap needed to address these threats proactively.
