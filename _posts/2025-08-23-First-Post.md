Welcome! My name is Anthony Perstin and I am Threat Hunter and Cyber Threat Intelligence analyst. I am passionate about cybersecurity, technology, and privacy. 

Cybersecurity is ultimately a fundamental mindset when it comes to working with technology. Technology (both software and hardware) is constantly being exploited for dozens of nefarious reasons and it our collective responsibility to proactivley protect againsts these threats to live in a more secure world.

My goal with this website is to share Threat Hunting, Detection Engineering, and Cyber Threat Intelligence (DEaTH + CTI) information with others to enable us to stay 1 step ahead in protecting our respective businesses, organizations and own personal digial assets. Additionally, I want to expand on including write-ups regarding red team methodologies, best practices that we can use everyday to better secure ourselves, and miscellaneous technology topics that interest me and may interest you. Because in order to protect against hackers, you have to know how to think like one.
